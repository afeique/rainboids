  ---
  Cost Balance Summary

  At ~300 coins/min and ~1 SP/2-3 min:
  - Wave 3 (~5 min): ~1500 coins, ~3 SP → can afford 1 cheap weapon OR 1 defense skill
  - Wave 5 (~10 min): ~4000 coins, ~5 SP → 1 primary + 1 power + a few upgrades
  - Wave 10 (~20 min): ~12000 coins, ~9 SP → full loadout but not max upgrades

  Players must specialize — they can't buy everything. This creates meaningful replayability and build diversity.
